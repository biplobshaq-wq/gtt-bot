# Game Time Tonight — daily European football policy

## Publishing rule
- Publish **at most one** upcoming European football match guide per America/New_York calendar day.
- Prioritise UEFA Champions League, UEFA Europa League, Premier League and other top European-league fixtures with broadly followed clubs.
- Use public schedule/score data only. If the data is unavailable or the next fixture cannot be verified, do not publish a guessed article.
- A completed match may update its original guide with a verified final score. It must not create a second thin result post.

## Original writing
- Every article is freshly written from the structured facts supplied to the writer.
- Do not copy or closely paraphrase news reports, previews, club copy, broadcaster copy, headlines, quotes or paywalled material.
- Do not invent injuries, form, transfers, standings, tactics, player details, odds, results or streaming availability.
- Clearly state when time, channel or coverage data is not listed, and tell readers to check official local listings.

## Copyright and embeds
- Do not upload, rebroadcast, frame or link to unauthorised live streams, highlights, broadcast clips or pirated video.
- Do not use club logos, player photos, press photographs or third-party broadcast graphics in the automated post.
- The automated image is an original text-and-shape graphic made by the bot. It contains no team badge, player likeness or footage.
- The match score box is the site's own dynamic presentation of public score data. It is not video.
- An official, share-enabled YouTube embed may be added only after a human verifies the exact official source and confirms that embedding is enabled. Never scrape or re-upload video.

## Reader trust
- Keep the existing disclosure that Game Time Tonight does not stream matches.
- Use original US-English copy that gives the kickoff time, listed coverage, venue and score-following information.
- Review errors promptly and correct a published post when verified schedule information changes.
